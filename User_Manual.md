# StyleSync User Manual

Welcome to **StyleSync**, the intelligent paraphrasing tool designed to copy your unique writing style perfectly!

This manual will guide you through setting up your style profile, viewing your writing guide, and using the tool to rewrite generic text in your own voice.

## 1. Account Creation and Sign In

To get started with StyleSync and ensure your profiles are saved, you need to sign in.
1. Open the StyleSync application.
2. Click the **Sign In** button located on the top navigation bar.
3. If you are a new user, follow the prompts to create your account. If you already have an account, simply log in.
*Note: If you are not signed in, some features will redirect you to the sign-in page to ensure your data is saved.*

## 2. Creating Your Style Profile

Before StyleSync can clone your writing, it needs to learn your "Style DNA". 

### The Best Way: Uploading an Essay (Recommended)
The most accurate way to teach StyleSync is to provide real examples of your writing.
1. Navigate to the **Onboarding / Style Profile** page (or click **Create Your Writing Style**).
2. Select the **Essay Upload Mode**.
3. Paste in 1 to 3 paragraphs of your typical writing.
   * **Tip for 100% Accuracy:** Provide at least 300–500 words. The more text you provide, the better the system learns your sentence rhythm, vocabulary level, contraction usage, and preferred transitions.
4. Click **Analyze Style**. The system will extract your grammatical fingerprint (Lexical Density, Burstiness, Pronoun Usage, etc.).
5. Give your profile a memorable name (e.g., "Casual Blog Style").
6. Click **Create Profile** (or **Save**) to save it. Your profile syncs to the server since you are signed in.

### The Quick Way: Questionnaire Mode
If you don't have a writing sample handy, you can answer a few quick questions.
1. Select the **Questionnaire Mode**.
2. Use the sliders to choose your Formality, Sentence Length, Vocabulary complexity, and Tone.
3. Click **Complete Questionnaire**.
4. The system will generate a synthetic writing sample based on your choices. Give the profile a name and save it. *(Note: This will not be as accurate as providing your own writing!)*

## 3. The Writing Guide

Once you have created a profile (especially via Essay Upload), you can access personalized insights.
1. Open the **Writing Guide** section.
2. You will see two tabs: **Essay Analysis** and **Writing Lessons**.
   - **Essay Analysis:** Provides detailed metrics and personalized feedback on your writing style.
   - **Writing Lessons:** Offers short, actionable tips and examples to help you improve your writing immediately.

## 4. Paraphrasing Text

Once your profile is set up, you can start rewriting text to sound exactly like you.

1. Go to the **Paraphrase** page.
2. On the left side, select your active **Style Profile** (it may be selected automatically).
3. Paste the generic text you want to rewrite into the input box.
4. Click **Paraphrase**.

### What Happens Behind the Scenes?
StyleSync doesn't just swap words. It runs a 10-dimension verification process. 
- It matches your sentence length variations (rhythm).
- It mirrors your punctuation habits (like how often you use commas or dashes).
- It applies your contraction rules strictly (e.g., always using "don't" instead of "do not" if you prefer it).
- If the AI's first attempt doesn't score at least a **75/100** on matching your style, it automatically corrects itself up to two times before showing you the final result!

## 5. Reviewing the Output

After paraphrasing, the output will appear on the right side.

### The Style Diagnostics Panel
Below the output, you will see a detailed breakdown:
- **Sentence Count & Length:** Compares the output to your natural average.
- **Unique Vocabulary:** Shows how complex the wording is.
- **Lexicon Hits:** Highlights signature words and phrases that the AI correctly carried over from your writing sample.

If you aren't satisfied with the result, you can use the **Writing Suggestions** panel or re-run the paraphrase.

## 6. Troubleshooting & Tips

- **Output sounds too formal?** Make sure your uploaded writing sample is casual. The AI strictly clones what you feed it.
- **AI-sounding phrases are appearing?** StyleSync automatically strips out cliche AI phrases (like "It is important to note"), but if some slip through, adding more of your own writing to your profile helps drown them out.
- **Need to preserve facts?** Don't worry! The system has a strict content-preservation engine. It will not delete dates, numbers, or proper nouns from the original text.
